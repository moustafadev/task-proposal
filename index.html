Introduction

Phase 1 delivers TASK as a complete, production-ready platform, not a demo: the customer app and the captain app (Android and iOS), the backend, and the admin dashboard, ready for real customers and real payments. Development takes 8–10 weeks, for a total of USD 5,000.

TASK connects customers in Iraq with verified service providers (captains) such as electricians, plumbers, cleaners and maintenance technicians. Customers pick a service, book a captain, pay in cash or online, and rate the job.

The project will be delivered by Mostafa, a developer with over 4 years of commercial experience in Flutter, backend development, API and payment gateway integration. A recent project is SuperApp, a multi-service marketplace (shopping, food ordering, delivery, chat) live on the App Store and Google Play, with a website at superap.ru.

Phase 1 Scope

All parts share one backend and database, with full Arabic and English support and a right-to-left interface.

1. Customer App (Android & iOS)

  • Sign up and log in with phone number and OTP verification
  • Browse service categories and sub-categories, and search
  • Find captains by location, rating, price, experience and availability
  • Create a request with description, photos, map location and preferred time
  • Pay online through an Iraqi payment gateway, or in cash on completion
  • Track booking status, chat with the captain in real time
  • Rate and review the captain, submit complaints
  • Push notifications, booking history, saved addresses, profile

2. Captain App (Android & iOS)

  • Registration with ID and certificate upload for admin verification
  • Offer multiple services from detailed categories, with a price for each service
  • Set working areas and availability
  • Receive bookings and inspection requests, send price quotations
  • Update job status and chat with the customer
  • Balance statement: jobs, earnings, commission owed and payouts
  • ZainCash wallet number for receiving payouts

3. Booking System

  • Instant bookings
  • Scheduled bookings
  • Inspection requests: the captain visits and assesses the job first
  • Price quotations from the captain, with customer approval before work starts

4. Financial System

  • Automatic commission calculation per job, with rates set from the dashboard by category or by captain
  • Captain balances: earnings, commission owed and payouts
  • Cash transactions tracked, with automatic account suspension when unpaid commission passes a set limit
  • Online payments through an Iraqi payment gateway (see Payment Gateway options below)
  • Automated bulk payouts of captain earnings to their ZainCash wallets, with status tracking
  • Settlements recorded and marked as paid from the dashboard
  • Financial reports: revenue, commissions, payouts and outstanding balances by period, city and captain
  • Every money movement recorded in a financial ledger, so the numbers always match and can be audited

5. Admin Dashboard (Web)

  • Manage customers and captains, verify captain documents
  • Manage categories, services, cities and commission rates
  • Monitor bookings, payments, settlements, complaints and reviews
  • Financial reports and platform statistics
  • Discount codes and broadcast notifications
  • Role-based access for the admin team

Scalable Architecture & Future Phases

Nothing built in Phase 1 is temporary. The same apps and backend grow with TASK.

  • New services, categories and cities are added from the admin dashboard, with no development needed.
  • The backend is modular, so each new feature is a separate, independent piece.
  • The financial ledger is designed so that wallets, subscription plans, referral rewards and new payment methods are added as extensions, not rewrites.

Phase 2 (after launch, based on real usage)

  • In-app wallets with top-ups, automatic refunds and withdrawals
  • Captain subscription plans as an alternative to commission
  • Referral program and loyalty points
  • Smarter matching using response time, completion rate and complaint history
  • Additional payment gateways

Phase 3 (growth)

  • AI features: describe a problem by voice or photo and get the right service and an estimated price
  • Expansion to new cities and categories
  • Kurdish language support

Future phases are quoted separately when planned.

Payment Gateway Options

Both options include full integration, testing in the gateway's test environment, server-side verification of every payment (no payment is confirmed unless the gateway confirms it), and automated bulk payouts to captains with status tracking. The client chooses the option before signing.

Option 1: ZainCash only (recommended for launch)

  • Customers pay from their ZainCash wallets.
  • Captains receive their earnings through ZainCash bulk payouts, sent to all captains at once.
  • One integration for both payments and payouts, with published merchant fees (0.6%).
  • Limitation: customers without a ZainCash wallet pay cash at launch. Card payments via Qi Card can be added in Phase 2.

Option 2: Qi Card for payments + ZainCash for payouts

  • Customers pay with Visa, Mastercard and Qi cards, or the SuperQi wallet, from day one.
  • Captains receive their earnings through ZainCash bulk payouts.
  • Limitation: two integrations instead of one, which adds development time. Customer payments arrive through Qi Card, so the ZainCash business wallet must be topped up before each payout batch.

Each captain needs a ZainCash wallet to receive payouts. Gateway fees, bulk payout fees and settlement times should be confirmed in writing with each provider.

المقدمة

تُسلِّم المرحلة الأولى منصة TASK كمنتج كامل جاهز للتشغيل الفعلي وليس نسخة تجريبية: تطبيق العملاء وتطبيق الكباتن (Android و iOS) والخادم الخلفي ولوحة تحكم الإدارة، جاهزة لعملاء حقيقيين ومدفوعات حقيقية. مدة التطوير 8–10 أسابيع، بتكلفة إجمالية 5,000 دولار أمريكي.

TASK تربط العملاء في العراق بمزوّدي خدمات موثّقين (الكباتن)، مثل الكهربائي والسبّاك وعامل التنظيف وفني الصيانة. يختار العميل الخدمة ويحجز الكابتن ويدفع نقداً أو أونلاين ثم يقيّم العمل.

سيتولى التنفيذ مصطفى، مطوّر بخبرة تجارية تزيد على 4 سنوات في Flutter وتطوير الخوادم وربط الـ API وبوابات الدفع. من أحدث أعماله تطبيق SuperApp، وهو منصة خدمات متعددة (تسوّق، طلب طعام، توصيل، محادثة) منشورة على App Store و Google Play، مع موقع ويب على superap.ru.

خيارات بوابة الدفع

يشمل الخياران الربط الكامل، والاختبار في بيئة التجربة الخاصة بالبوابة، والتحقق من كل عملية دفع من الخادم (لا تُعتمد أي عملية إلا بعد تأكيد البوابة)، والصرف الجماعي التلقائي للكباتن مع متابعة الحالة. يختار العميل الخيار قبل التوقيع.

الخيار الأول: ZainCash فقط (المقترح للإطلاق)

  • يدفع العملاء من محافظ ZainCash.
  • يستلم الكباتن مستحقاتهم عبر الصرف الجماعي من ZainCash لجميع الكباتن دفعة واحدة.
  • ربط واحد للدفع والصرف معاً، برسوم تاجر منشورة (0.6%).
  • القيد: العميل الذي لا يملك محفظة ZainCash يدفع نقداً عند الإطلاق، ويمكن إضافة الدفع بالبطاقات عبر Qi Card في المرحلة الثانية.

الخيار الثاني: Qi Card للدفع + ZainCash للصرف

  • يدفع العملاء ببطاقات Visa و Mastercard و Qi أو بمحفظة SuperQi من اليوم الأول.
  • يستلم الكباتن مستحقاتهم عبر الصرف الجماعي من ZainCash.
  • القيد: ربطان بدلاً من ربط واحد، مما يضيف وقتاً للتطوير. وبما أن مدفوعات العملاء تصل عبر Qi Card، يجب شحن محفظة ZainCash التجارية قبل كل دفعة صرف.

يحتاج كل كابتن إلى محفظة ZainCash لاستلام مستحقاته. يُفضّل تأكيد رسوم البوابة ورسوم الصرف الجماعي ومدة التحويل كتابياً مع كل مزوّد.
